# Provenance and model card

Curated from the existing Final_Gun_Violence project. Original files and environments remain unchanged. Public notebook outputs were cleared. Full data, fitted models and scenario databases are excluded from Git. The local source manifest records original source hashes outside the repository.

Research authors: Asish Satpathy, Vinay Vikkranth Subramanian and Avantika Tiwari, as credited by the AMCIS 2026 proceedings record. Source inspection supports implementation and recorded results, not sole authorship of every line or an independent fresh replication.

Intended use: research inspection, method comparison and discussion of spatial generalization. Unsupported uses: individual risk scoring, causal policy recommendations, calibrated forecasting, law enforcement targeting or an automatically deployable public service.

The source records zero-population rows and some percentage features above 100; inspect these rather than treating all numeric values as valid measurements. Scores are uncalibrated model outputs. Global random-split results and geographic holdouts are separate experiments. The public synthetic example adds a clean demonstration of group isolation and train-only preprocessing without rewriting historical findings.
